Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers the Five Star Chaos Uploader ("the app"), an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Five Star Chaos.

What data the app accesses

Through Google OAuth, and only for the channel owner's own Google account, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and change their privacy status;
  • read public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own videos;
  • read YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained) of the owner's own channel.

The app does not access other users' accounts or channels and does not collect personal data from viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to decide which kind of content performs better. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared or transferred to third parties, except that video frames and text may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) to help write titles and review videos before publishing.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops all API use. The owner can delete the locally stored tokens and statistics at any time; stored data is deleted within 30 days after access is revoked.
